Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our delivery service

What information do I need to provide when booking? −
To process your booking efficiently, please include the following details: Required dates Collection and delivery addresses (including postcodes) Contact details (emails and phone numbers) Item description (including dimensions) Your preferred delivery option
Do I need to specify exact dates? +
Yes, please select the required dates when placing your booking so we can plan the route accordingly.
What should I include in the address details? +
Please provide full collection and delivery addresses, including postcodes. If one of the addresses is a business, mention it so we can accommodate working hours. If additional details are needed (e.g. lot number, name), please include them.
How do I choose a delivery option? +
You can select one of the 4 available delivery options during booking. Make sure to review what is included in each option before choosing.
What contact details are required? +
We need contact details for both collection and delivery, including phone numbers and email addresses.
What should I include in the item description? +
Please provide a clear description of the item, including its dimensions, to help us plan the transport properly.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept card payments at the time of booking.
What does “Leave in a secure place” mean? +
This option allows the courier to: Collect the item from a secure location, or Leave the item in a secure place upon delivery Please provide clear instructions describing where the item is or should be left.
How are delivery routes planned? +
Routes are created daily and finalized by noon. Our logistics team optimizes routes to meet all customers' needs as efficiently as possible.
How will I receive updates about my delivery? +
Notifications are sent based on the delivery option selected during booking.
Why do some deliveries take 2 days? +
Some routes, especially to Scotland and remote areas, are planned 2–3 days in advance due to distance and logistics. Typically: Day 1: Deliveries Day 2: Collections This allows couriers to operate efficiently and maximize capacity.
What happens if my order is rescheduled? +
Your order will be prioritized and typically completed within the next 2–3 days.
